# Pronunciation And Voice QA Notes

## Style

- Mandarin main voice.
- Natural business-documentary tone.
- Avoid robotic pauses between every technical noun.
- Prefer slightly slower delivery for decision criteria and risk terms.

## Terms

| Written Term | Preferred Spoken Form | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Agent | AI Agent / 智能体 | Use `智能体` if the TTS makes English sound unnatural. |
| Workflow | 工作流 | Use Chinese unless the English term is visually important. |
| Sequential Workflow | 顺序工作流 | Avoid spelling each English syllable. |
| Single Agent | 单智能体 | Use Chinese in narration; keep English on screen. |
| Multi-Agent | 多智能体 | Use Chinese in narration; keep English on screen. |
| Harness | 运行护栏 / Harness | First say `运行护栏` if English pronunciation sounds awkward. |
| CRM | C-R-M | Spell letters clearly. |

## QA Checklist

- No word should sound like a machine-read English acronym unless intentional.
- `Agent` must not be pronounced with a hard awkward pause before or after it.
- `Workflow`, `Single Agent`, and `Multi-Agent` should be understandable to non-technical business viewers.
- Subtitles must match the final spoken meaning, not necessarily every filler word.
- Final timing must come from measured audio, not estimated scene durations.
