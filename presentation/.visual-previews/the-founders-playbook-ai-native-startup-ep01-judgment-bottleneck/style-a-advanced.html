Rounded/humanist Chinese sans for voice-facing text; mono only for source/status marks. No serif subtitles.

Graphite base. Amber for decision/risk, cyan for structure/focus, red only for wrong direction.

Artifacts

Paths, gates, lanes, controls. No fake dashboards or unsupported metrics.

Motion

Reveal, path bend, lane activation, gate shift, focus highlight. One motion idea per chunk.
