Three initial style directions plus a rebuilt Style A board that separates confirmed visual identity from content-specific scene design. These are previews, not Remotion implementation.

A. Founder Decision Gate

Dark, precise, decision-led. Best fit for the episode’s core metaphor and future motion states.

B. Source Editorial Ledger

Credible, report-like, source-aware. Strong for trust, weaker for short-form energy.

C. Warm Cognitive Field

Warmer and more emotional. Strong hook energy, but needs discipline to avoid decorative motion.
Recommended direction: A as the production base, rebuilt with VI as the shared layer and content-fit modules as the scene layer.
