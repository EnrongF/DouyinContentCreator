## Episode Position

Episode 4 covers the Launch stage.

Episode 3 asks how to build a focused MVP without drift. Episode 4 asks whether the product and company can grow without the founder personally holding every loop.

```text
MVP: prove the product deserves to exist.
Launch: prove the business deserves to grow.
```

## Episode Promise

Help the viewer see that launch readiness is not just traffic, users, or shipping. It is replacing repeated founder attention with repeatable systems.

One-sentence Chinese promise:

```text
上线以后，增长不能靠创始人把每件事都亲自接住。
```

## Exact Mental Upgrade

Founder-in-the-loop is useful during Idea and MVP because tight judgment loops matter. At Launch, the same habit becomes a bottleneck when support, triage, sprint planning, reporting, and repeated decisions only move when the founder personally touches them.

Compressed mental upgrade:

```text
Launch 不是让创始人更忙，而是把重复发生的问题变成系统。
```

## Target Audience

- Founder moving from early traction to repeatable growth.
- Solo builder whose support, bug triage, reporting, or product decisions all route through them.
- Startup operator designing lightweight processes around an AI-assisted product.
- Technical founder preparing for production users.

## Target Duration

Recommended target: `60-75 seconds`.

Reason:
- The episode needs one clear launch-stage mental upgrade plus a practical bottleneck audit.
- It should not become a full production readiness, compliance, and growth metrics tutorial.

## Episode Spine

1. MVP proves the product deserves to exist; Launch proves the business deserves to grow.
2. Founder attention is useful early, but becomes a launch bottleneck.
3. Warning signs: hour-long decisions take a week, support piles up, recurring tasks happen only when the founder remembers.
4. Practical audit: what can be automated, delegated, or still requires founder judgment?
5. Takeaway: build systems that free founder attention for decisions only the founder can make.

## Retained Source Value

- Launch stage goal: turn early traction into repeatable, sustainable growth.
- Launch exit criteria include repeatable channel-driven growth, production workload readiness, and operations without founder bottlenecks.
- The goal is not to remove the founder, but to free attention for founder-only decisions.
- Founder-in-every-loop changes from asset to constraint.
- Bottleneck audit should identify what can be systematized, delegated, or still merits founder time.
- Lightweight processes should run without requiring founder intervention to trigger.

## Non-Cuttable Claims And Caveats

- Do not imply the founder should disappear. The source says free founder attention for decisions only the founder can make.
- Launch is not Scale. Keep the episode about repeatable growth and operational systems, not defensibility.
- Operations without founder bottlenecks is one of the source exit criteria.
- Production readiness matters, but this episode should not expand into a technical compliance checklist.

## Compression

### Core Claim

```text
上线以后，创始人的任务不是把每个问题都接住，而是把重复发生的问题变成系统。
```

### Compact Mental Model

```text
创始人不能当中转站。
```

Meaning:
- Early on, founder involvement creates speed and learning.
- At launch, support, triage, reporting, and recurring decisions must not depend on founder memory.
- The founder should design the system and keep only the decisions that genuinely require founder judgment.
