## Extracted Visual Tokens

- Report identity: professional enterprise AI guide, not consumer or creator-first.
- Dominant source artifact style: light-background architecture diagrams with
  simple boxes, connectors, labels, and muted fills.
- Diagram typography: small English labels; source labels should remain visible
  when diagrams are used as evidence.
- Shape language: rectangles, arrows, supervisor/workflow boxes, tool/memory
  boxes, fan-out/fan-in paths, evaluator loops.
- Useful project overlay palette: graphite/off-white base, cyan for active
  explanation, amber for decision/cost thresholds, red for risk caveats.
- Motion fit: source diagrams support annotation, crop/zoom, outline, progressive
  highlight, and montage; they do not support heavy decorative transitions.

## Endorsement / Fidelity Risks

- Do not use Anthropic branding as channel branding.
- Do not imply Anthropic endorsed the series or the video.
- Keep attribution as source mark only: `Source: Anthropic PDF / Building Effective AI Agents`.
- Do not redraw the source diagrams into a new taxonomy.
- Do not make `hierarchical`, `sequential`, and `parallel` look like peers.
- If diagrams are recombined, label the result as synthesis or composition.
- Do not turn `emerging patterns` into production best practice.

## Recommendation To Visual System Designer

Build EP06 previews around three serious directions before locking style:

1. `source-native` / `source-editorial-technical`: source diagrams dominate, with
   Chinese annotation and source attribution.
2. `decision-matrix`: the episode becomes a sequence of evidence gates:
   single agent, routing, specialists, multi-agent, evaluator, emerging risk.
3. `executive-technical-documentary` with restrained `systems-control-room`
   inserts: strongest if Douyin retention needs more motion and operational
   contrast.

Do not implement Remotion changes until one direction is approved.
