## Mental Upgrade

上线以后，创始人的任务不是把每个问题都接住，而是把重复发生的问题变成系统。

## Audience

- Founder moving from MVP to Launch.
- Solo builder whose support, bug triage, reporting, or repeated decisions all route through them.
- AI-native startup operator designing lightweight launch processes.

## Value Budget

Retained:
- Launch is about repeatable business growth, not only product existence.
- Founder attention becomes a bottleneck when every loop routes through the founder.
- The practical audit is automation, delegation, and founder-only judgment.

Moved:
- Scale moat and workflow defensibility.
- Enterprise readiness, security, compliance, and detailed growth metrics.

Non-cuttable caveat:
- The founder should not disappear. Founder time is reserved for direction, tradeoff, and risk judgment.
