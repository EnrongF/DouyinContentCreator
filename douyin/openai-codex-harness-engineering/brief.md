# Brief

## Core Thesis

OpenAI's article is not mainly about "AI writes code." It is about a new engineering operating system:

> human attention becomes the scarce resource; agents become the execution layer; harness engineering becomes the discipline that makes that execution legible, bounded, testable, and maintainable.

## Horizontal Wide Angle

Most people will summarize this article as "Codex wrote one million lines of code." That misses the important part.

The more useful compression:

- OpenAI did not just use agents.
- They redesigned the repository, tooling, observability, architecture, review, and cleanup loops so agents could operate.
- Editorial compression: the moat is less about prompt skill alone, and more about the harness around the agent.

## Audience

- AI builders
- technical founders
- engineering managers
- enterprise AI operators
- agentic system builders

## Visual Strategy

- Style: Executive Technical Documentary.
- Mood: restrained, source-faithful, institutional.
- Keep the original numbers, diagrams, and checklists visible.
