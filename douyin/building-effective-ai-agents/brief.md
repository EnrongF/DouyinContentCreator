# Douyin Brief

## Positioning

This video is a business implementation guide for AI agents. It translates Anthropic's agent architecture material into a decision map for business users.

## Audience

- Business leaders.
- Product owners.
- Digital transformation teams.
- Consultants.
- AI implementation decision-makers.
- Technical managers who need to explain architecture choice to non-engineers.

## Core Question

什么时候该用 Agent？该用哪种 Agent？什么时候不要用？

## Core Promise

帮业务用户判断：一个业务问题到底需要自动化、Workflow、单 Agent、多 Agent，还是带 Harness 的生产系统。

## Visual Direction

- Native 16:9 executive technical documentary.
- Burned-in subtitles in the MP4.
- Consistent cover.
- One large business claim per scene.
- One clear architecture artifact per scene.
