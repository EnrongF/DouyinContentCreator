# OpenAI TTS Pronunciation Notes

## Voice Goal
- Natural conversational Mandarin.
- Short-video technology explainer style.
- Clear but not broadcast-host style.
- Slightly fast pace, with firm pauses around English technical terms.

## Terms To Preserve
- AI: read as English letters.
- Coding Agent: keep as English phrase.
- Delivery Agent: keep as English phrase.
- Harness engineering: keep as English phrase.
- delivery harness: keep as English phrase.
- software delivery knowledge graph: keep as English phrase.
- feature flag: keep as English phrase.
- scorecard, rollback, audit log: keep as English terms.
- API key: read as English letters plus "key".
- RAG: read as English letters.
- Knowledge Graph: keep as English phrase.
- Delegate: keep as English word.
- Kubernetes: keep as English word.
- MCP Server: keep as English letters plus Server.
- toolsets: keep as English word.
- on-call: keep as English phrase.

## Quality Bar
- English terms should sound intentional, not randomly translated.
- Mandarin should sound like a person explaining an engineering idea, not a system announcement.
- If a generated take sounds too stiff, regenerate with another voice: `marin`, `cedar`, `coral`, then `nova`.
